\section{Introduction}
College life is so much more than just studying well for CGPA, its a complicated mess that 
can be impossible to untangle once in. 

Through this paper I want to untangle one such mess 
regarding the open shared space in front of the Sports Block building, is it the Football Ground? the Cricket Ground?
the badminton ground? the volleyball ground? the place to connect at, evolve at, improve at, change at, play at, rest at, and what not, 
its the very nature of humans to find green grass and try to touch it and feel its texture. 

Through this paper i just want to figure out, what role does the sports block park play in an average lad's college life.
